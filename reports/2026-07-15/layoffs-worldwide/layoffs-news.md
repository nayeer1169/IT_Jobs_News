## Global Situation

Workforce reductions in 2026 are heavily characterized by a continuous stream of smaller, incremental restructuring actions (sometimes referred to as the "Forever Layoff Era") rather than single, dramatic events. Driven by AI integration, automated workflows, and high capital expenditure (capex) costs for AI infrastructure, enterprises are reducing positions in middle management, sales, customer support, and manual quality assurance.

**Roles at higher risk:**
- Middle management and non-technical coordinators
- Entry-level code maintenance and manual QA
- Regional administrative support and legacy consulting
- Xbox gaming division support roles

**Roles less exposed:**
- Machine learning research and MLOps practitioners
- Cloud infrastructure architects and SREs
- Cybersecurity specialists and defensive engineers
- Data platform engineers managing large-scale ETL pipelines
