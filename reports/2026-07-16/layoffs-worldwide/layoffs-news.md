## Global Situation

Workforce reductions in 2026 are heavily characterized by a continuous stream of smaller, incremental restructuring actions (referred to as the "Forever Layoff Era") rather than single, dramatic events. Driven by AI integration, automated workflows, and high capital expenditure (capex) costs for AI infrastructure, enterprises are reducing positions in middle management, sales, customer support, and manual quality assurance.

**Roles at higher risk:**
- Middle management and non-technical coordinators
- Entry-level code maintenance and manual QA
- Legacy database administration and system support
- Regional administrative support and legacy consulting

**Roles less exposed:**
- Machine learning research and MLOps practitioners
- Cloud infrastructure architects and SREs (Site Reliability Engineers)
- Prompt engineers and AI safety researchers
- Data engineers specializing in real-time pipelines

## India Situation

The Indian IT sector is experiencing a rise in "silent layoffs," where headcounts are reduced through performance management, attrition, or silent restructuring without public announcements of mass terminations. While IT services firms remain cautious, Global Capability Centers (GCCs) continue to show resilience, serving as a buffer and hiring targeted talent in emerging regional hubs.
