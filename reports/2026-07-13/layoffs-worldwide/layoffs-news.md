## Global Situation

The global tech sector is undergoing an AI-first restructuring phase. Rather than massive, sudden layoffs across entire organizations, companies are implementing "silent layoffs"—performance-linked trims, hiring freezes in non-essential areas, and ongoing team realignments. Companies are funding expensive GPU clusters and AI R&D by streamlining middle management, administrative, and traditional engineering teams.

**Roles at higher risk:**
- Middle management and non-technical coordinators
- Entry-level code maintenance and manual QA
- Regional administrative support and legacy consulting
- Non-AI software development and traditional sales coordination

**Roles less exposed:**
- Machine learning research and MLOps practitioners
- Cloud infrastructure architects and SREs
- Cybersecurity specialists and defensive engineers
- Data platform engineers managing large-scale ETL pipelines

## India Situation

The Indian IT services sector is transitioning away from mass recruitments, with major firms emphasizing efficiency and AI adaptability. There is a rise in silent layoffs and restructuring actions. For example, US-based healthcare analytics firm CorroHealth recently initiated layoffs affecting nearly 800 employees at its Kerala centers, which triggered state labor commission intervention. Conversely, Global Capability Centers (GCCs) in major hubs like Bengaluru and Hyderabad continue to show resilience, absorbing specialized talent.
